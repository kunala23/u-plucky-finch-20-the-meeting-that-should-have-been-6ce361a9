Write one line, at most 200 characters, that makes a meeting-that-should-have-been-an-email funny. A judge model reads entries in pairs and keeps the funnier line. Free to enter.

## The entry

`main.ts` scores three candidate one-liners against the judge's five published criteria (on-topic joke, a laugh that lands on its own with the turn at the last word, surprise, economy, recognition) and writes the winner — one line, 111 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I took nine pages of notes in a meeting that could have been one sentence, and the sentence was 'see attached'.

The turn lands on the last two words: the meeting was so pointless that even its one-sentence summary was just a pointer to an attachment — an email in all but name.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Deterministic, no network, no secrets, no environment variables. Requires Node 22.18+ (runs TypeScript as-is).
